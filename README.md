About PromptPilot

PromptPilot is a desktop application designed to help creators build high quality prompts for AI image and video generation.

Built with ComfyUI creators in mind, PromptPilot uses locally installed Ollama models to create detailed, model aware prompts.

Your ideas and prompts remain on your own computer.

    No cloud processing required
    No subscriptions
    No API keys
    No telemetry
    No tracking
    Local AI powered by Ollama

PromptPilot Version 3.1

PromptPilot 3.1 is the latest evolution of the PromptPilot workspace.

Version 3.1 expands PromptPilot with a dedicated Video Generator, improved video prompting, target specific video formatting, dialogue and audio controls, reference image guidance, and improved continuity tools while preserving the redesigned workspace, AI Engine, Workflow Studio, Style Library, expanded AI model support, image prompting, and ComfyUI creator tools introduced in Version 3.

The goal remains the same:

Make powerful AI prompting easier without making the software complicated.
PromptPilot AI Engine

PromptPilot's embedded AI Engine helps turn simple ideas into detailed prompts while showing you what is happening during generation.

The AI Engine provides:

    Live progress

    Generation percentage

    Current processing stage

    Elapsed time

    PromptPilot tips

    Completion summary

    Model aware prompt generation

    Image and video prompt optimization

Everything is integrated directly into Prompt Builder.
Model Aware Prompt Generation

PromptPilot does more than expand a description.

It understands which AI platform or model you are targeting and adjusts the prompt accordingly.

This allows PromptPilot to create prompts specifically for image generators, video generators, ComfyUI models, and general AI systems.

Video Generator

PromptPilot 3.1 introduces a dedicated Video Generator for creating structured prompts for AI video generation.

Video Generator includes:

    Prompt to Video

    Image to Video

    Start Frame

    Subject

    Subject Action

    Environment Motion

    Camera Movement

    Motion Strength

    Lighting and visual tone

    End Frame

    Dialogue

    Voice direction

    Music

    Sound effects

    Reference image guidance

    Subject and environment consistency controls

    Chronological shot construction

    Target specific video prompt formatting

PromptPilot can preserve intentionally still subjects, locked cameras, stable environments, lighting, framing, and ending states when the requested shot does not require movement.

When movement is requested, PromptPilot can organize subject action, camera movement, environmental response, and the ending state into a coherent video prompt.
Video AI Targets

PromptPilot 3.1 supports target specific prompting for a wide range of AI video systems including Sora, Seedance, WAN, Veo, Kling, Runway, MiniMax H3, LTX Video, Hailuo AI, Mochi, Stable Video Diffusion, FramePack, HunyuanVideo, CogVideoX, AnimateDiff, Pika, PixVerse, Haiper, Luma Dream Machine, Ray2, Vidu, CapCut AI Video, Kaiber, Genmo, Open Sora, Adobe Firefly Video, and additional supported targets.
Dialogue and Audio

Video prompts can include dialogue, voice direction, music, environmental ambience, and sound effects when requested.

PromptPilot can also explicitly preserve a silent video with no dialogue, narration, voiceover, music, or sound effects.
Reference Image Video Prompting

When a reference image is used, PromptPilot can preserve the visible subject design, face, clothing, proportions, composition, environment, lighting, color, camera style, framing, and opening frame.
Style Library

PromptPilot 3 introduces an expanded Style Library for quickly adding visual styles to your prompts.

Select a style and send it directly to Prompt Builder.

The Style Library is designed to make experimenting with different visual directions faster and easier without requiring you to manually write complex style descriptions.

Installation

<details> <summary><b>Windows Installation</b></summary>

<br>

    Download PromptPilot_v3.1_Windows_Setup.exe.

    Run the installer.

    Choose your preferred installation location.

    Complete the installation.

    Launch PromptPilot.

    PromptPilot will connect to your local Ollama installation when local AI features are used.

    Launch PromptPilot:

./PromptPilot_3.1_x86_64.AppImage
